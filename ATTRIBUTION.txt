Models, data and assets
- No app-configured external LLM or paid speech API is called.
- Browser SpeechRecognition may use an OS/vendor service; availability and terms vary. No acoustic model is bundled.
- AI note imports explicitly identify an unverified upstream model and require synthetic data. Seed summaries are authored fixtures.
- Importance ranking, limited conflict patterns, PHI patterns and the EN/ZH keyword glossary are project code, not trained medical models.
- All included care records, names, IDs and source conversations are synthetic. No real patient material or external imagery is included.
- Unicode symbols and CSS UI; no external icon pack or font file.
